Access and Troubleshooting

Detection runs automatically in permitted sites’ tabs. Chrome needs access to both the page and media/frame origins. A CDN grant alone does not enable other pages.

If a player was already loaded, rescan or restart playback. Some frames may need a page reload after granting access.

Thumbnails may need CDN access. Thumbnails use no cookies and may be unavailable. Detection does not guarantee download; sources can expire or require a session.

Settings and Privacy

Media candidates expire after ten minutes and clear on navigation, tab closure or browser session end. Site grants remain until revoked. Only your theme preference is stored durably.

No cookies, passwords, Authorization headers, telemetry, response bodies or media caches are collected. Remove the extension through Chrome’s Extensions page to remove extension-owned data; this does not promise secure erasure of backups or caches. Desktop bridge resources remain app-owned. Downloads and desktop history are unaffected.

Clear data removes temporary sources; detection continues on permitted sites and may rediscover current players.
